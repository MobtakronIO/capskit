## Development Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** Code should be readable by the next person (or AI) who looks at it.
2. **Plan before code.** Every feature starts with a spec, not a prompt.
3. **Small, verifiable steps.** Break work into tasks that can be individually tested.
4. **Document decisions.** Record why, not just what.
